# CareRide

Scheduled medical rides for elderly patients, booked by family from another city. A self-initiated product strategy case study by Sanjog Kukreja: how a ride-hailing platform could enter non-emergency medical transport in India.

- **Interactive prototype:** `index.html`. Step through one week of rides from both phones, the caregiver's and the rider's, with what the system does and why at each moment.
- **Caregiver app (Neha's phone):** `caregiver/`
- **Rider app (Sunanda's phone, simple mode):** `rider/`
- Open the two apps in the same browser and they stay in sync: set up the rides in the caregiver app and step through the week from either one.
- The case study covers the problem and evidence, users, RICE prioritisation, scope, metrics and risks.

The family, drivers, hospital and numbers in the demo are fictional; market figures are sourced on the page.
